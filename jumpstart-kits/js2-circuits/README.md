# JS2 — Circuit Construction and Circuit Optimization

**On the roadmap — not yet available.**

Picks up the Versal Premium FPGA built in JS1 and carries one GTM transceiver quad from silicon
to the board edge: escaping ten differential pairs out of a 1,369-ball, 0.92 mm grid on the JS1
HDI stackup, then landing them on a high-speed connector. Construction builds the escape and lane
circuits in code; optimization tunes the launch — antipads, via fences, deskew — against
full-wave simulation. Follows [JS1](../js1-stackup-components/).
