FABRICATION NOTES: jitxexamples.jumpstart_kits.js5_diplexer.main.Diplexer
(JLCPCB, 2-layer RO4350B 0.76 mm)

These notes travel with the fabrication packet of this board, exported with
jitxlib-jlcpcb from the design as committed beside it: re-export the packet
after any change to the board. The JLCPCB export cannot carry the notes: the
gerber set has no notes or drawing layer, and the order sheet (the packet's
-order.md file) has no via-covering or note field. Keep this file beside the
order sheet, and enter note 1's first sentence in the order form's remark
field. The order sheet's "Stackup: Proxied" line is an exporter fault: the
stackup is jitxlib.jlcpcb's JLC02076_RO4350B.

1. IN-PAD VIAS: the ground vias in the edge-launch jacks' ground pads
   (J1, J2, J3; 0.30 mm drill, 0.45 mm pad, 10 per jack) are plated and
   OPEN: not filled, not capped, not tented. JLCPCB does not fill or cap
   vias on its Rogers boards. Solder may wick into them when the jacks are
   soldered; that is accepted. Order form: pick no filled, capped or plugged
   Via Covering. The mask gerber opens the 30 in-pad vias on both sides and
   covers every other via. Which remaining Via Covering option follows the
   mask gerber on JLCPCB's Rogers form is not confirmed here: ask JLCPCB
   before ordering.

2. TOP SOLDERMASK is opened over the RF signal lines, their coplanar gaps
   and the connector launches as drawn in the mask gerber. Do not add mask
   there.

3. BOARD EDGE: the jacks' centre and ground pads run to the board edge as
   the Würth 60312202114514 data sheet draws them. Every other copper keeps
   JLCPCB's 0.20 mm copper-to-edge minimum.

4. JACK BOTTOM TABS: the jack is made for a 1.6 mm board, so on this
   0.76 mm board its bottom ground tabs stand about 0.9 mm off the bottom
   ground pads and are left unsoldered, as the design accepts. The launch's
   ground is the top ground pads and the open in-pad vias down to the
   bottom pads. The bottom paste layer still draws those pads; the packet
   orders top-side assembly only, so no bottom stencil is needed.
